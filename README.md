## About

I am a dual-degree undergraduate pursuing a B.Sc. in Data Science and Applications at **IIT Madras** alongside a B.Sc. (Hons.) in Chemistry at the **University of Delhi**. My work centres on building predictive machine learning systems, NLP classifiers, and end-to-end data pipelines in Python, and on turning them into usable products.

- **Software engineering:** clean, testable Python with relational schema design and CRUD backends built end to end.
- **AI / ML:** gradient boosting, ensemble methods, feature engineering, and rigorous model evaluation.
- **Generative AI:** Gemini LLM APIs, prompt engineering, structured JSON schemas, and agentic workflows.
- **Full stack development:** Flask and SQLite applications covering planning, booking, and inventory workflows.
- **Product engineering mindset:** prototypes shipped under tight deadlines, including a 30-hour buildathon solo build.

**Open To**

- Internships and entry-level roles in machine learning, data science, and AI engineering
- Research collaborations and open-source contributions
- Hackathon teams and product-focused builds
